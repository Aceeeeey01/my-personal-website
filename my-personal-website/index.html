ABOUT

A little about how I work.

The short version: I care about craft, clarity, and the small details most people skip.

I'm Edrianne Jay Coles, a Frontend Developer with 2 years of experience turning ambiguous ideas into polished, usable products. My background spans design systems, motion, and full front-end implementation — which means I can take something from a rough sketch to a shipped interface without losing the original intent along the way.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of visual design and engineering: I like environments where a design decision and a technical constraint are actually the same conversation.
